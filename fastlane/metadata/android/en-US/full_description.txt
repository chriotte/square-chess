Square Chess is designed for compact Android devices, especially square and near-square screens.

The chessboard takes centre stage, using as much of the display as possible. If your phone has a physical QWERTY keyboard, you can also play by typing moves such as e4, Nf3 or e2e4.

Play against the computer at ten difficulty levels, from beginner-friendly play to full-strength Fairy-Stockfish. The engine runs entirely on your phone.

You can also play with a friend on the same device, use an optional chess clock, record games played on a real board, and review them afterwards move by move.

Square Chess supports both touch and keyboard input. Tap or drag pieces, or enter moves using standard chess notation such as e4, Nf3, O-O or e2e4.

Your games can be reviewed, undone, resigned or exported as PGN for use with Lichess, ChessBase and other chess software.

The app also includes a standalone chess clock, board colour options, legal-move indicators, move sounds and vibration.

No ads. No account. No Internet permission. Your games and settings stay on your device.

Square Chess is particularly suited to 1:1 and 9:10 displays, while still working on conventional Android screens.
