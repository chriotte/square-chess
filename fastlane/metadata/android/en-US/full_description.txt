Square Chess is a chess app built for Android devices that ordinary chess apps often overlook — square and near-square screens, e-ink devices, compact devices and devices with physical keyboards.

The board comes first. Square Chess uses as much of the display as possible, whether you are playing by touch, typing moves on a physical keyboard, or using an e-ink screen.

Play against the computer at ten difficulty levels, from beginner-friendly play to full strength. You can also enable hints when you want a little help without giving up control of the game.

Train your tactics with 10,000 offline puzzles. Work through a course of 27 short chapters from mate in one to endgames, pick one of 29 themes, play a quick set of five, or repeat a fixed set until the patterns stick. After a wrong move, the app explains what goes wrong. The puzzles you miss come back for review, and a daily goal keeps you going.

Follow your progress with a puzzle rating and a game rating for games against the computer, with a 30-day chart and your accuracy for every theme.

Turn on Playful extras for a more colourful game. Choose a world (Forest, Banana Jungle, Ocean or Space) and a buddy who cheers you on when you solve a puzzle and encourages you to try again after a mistake. Earn stickers for your first checkmate, puzzle streaks and wins, and unlock hats, swords, wands and pets to dress your buddy up. Playful extras are off by default, and e-ink mode stays calm black and white.

You can also:

• Play with a friend on the same device, with an optional chess clock.
• Set up any position and play it against the computer or a friend.
• Pick up where you left off from Home, with your recent games at a glance.
• Record games played on a real chessboard and review them afterwards.
• Type moves such as e4, Nf3, O-O or e2e4 on a physical keyboard.
• Use a dedicated e-ink mode with a clear black-and-white board, shape-based highlights and no animations.
• Review, undo and export your games as PGN for Lichess, ChessBase and other chess software.
• Use the built-in standalone chess clock for over-the-board games.

Everything runs locally on your device, and the app works fully offline.

No ads. No analytics. No account needed.

If you want your games, puzzles and ratings on more than one device, you can sign in to an optional squarechess.org account to sync them. The app uses the Internet only for this, and only after you sign in. Without an account, your games and settings stay on your device. You can also save everything to a backup file.

Square Chess works on conventional Android phones and tablets too, but is designed especially for devices where standard chess apps do not make the best use of the screen or hardware.
