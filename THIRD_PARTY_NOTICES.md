# Third-party artwork

Square Chess bundles the Fantasy chess-piece artwork by Maurizio Monge.

- Source project: https://github.com/maurimo/chess-art
- The source SVGs used for this build are also available in the Lichess piece collection: https://github.com/lichess-org/lila/tree/master/public/piece/fantasy
- Licence: MIT (full text in `app/src/main/assets/pieces/LICENSE.txt`)

The Android PNGs in `app/src/main/res/drawable-nodpi` are rasterized copies of
the corresponding source SVGs. The MIT licence permits commercial use and
redistribution, provided that the copyright and permission notice are kept.
