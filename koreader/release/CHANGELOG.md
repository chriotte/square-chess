# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — 2026-11-22

First release.

- Play Fairy-Stockfish at ten levels, as White, Black or a random colour, or two players on one device
- Time controls from 1+0 to 30+0, with a clock bar you tap to pause
- Hints drawn on the board, and optional evaluation with Best, Good, Inaccuracy, Mistake and Blunder labels
- Review a game move by move, and continue playing from any position in it
- 10,000 Lichess puzzles with adaptive training, themes, Quick 5, two-step hints and spaced review
- A chess clock for games on a real board, with the two halves facing each other
- Open and save PGN files
- Moves by tap, drag or typing, with pen input treated the same as touch
- An e-ink board: hatched dark squares, shape-based highlights, no animation
