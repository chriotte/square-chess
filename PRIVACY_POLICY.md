# Square Chess privacy policy

Last updated: 28 November 2026

This policy is published at https://squarechess.org/privacy.

Square Chess is published by DataEspresso (Christopher Ottesen, London, United Kingdom).

## Summary

Square Chess does not sell any personal data and has no advertising and no analytics. Everything stays on your device unless you choose to sign in to an optional squarechess.org account to sync your games between devices. Without an account, the app sends nothing.

## Data stored on your device

- **Games:** the moves, results, clock times and settings of the games you play or record.
- **Puzzles and ratings:** your puzzle progress, puzzle and game ratings, daily goal and puzzles per day, and repeat sets.
- **Preferences:** sound, vibration, board colour and similar display settings.

This data stays in the app's private storage on your device. Android cloud backup is turned off for the app. If you uninstall the app, Android deletes this data.

## Data you choose to share

The app sends nothing by itself. Data leaves your device only when you choose one of these actions:

- **Share or export games (PGN or FEN):** you select the destination, for example a file location, an email app or a cloud drive. The app that receives the file handles it under its own privacy policy.
- **Report a bug or request a feature:** the app prepares a message that you can read and change before you send it. Device diagnostics are included only if you turn them on. Your own email app sends the message to support@dataespresso.com. We use the message only to answer you and to improve Square Chess.

## Optional account and sync

Settings → Sign in to sync connects the app to a squarechess.org account. The app uses the Internet only for this, and only after you sign in.

- **Signing in** happens on squarechess.org in your browser. You type your password there, not in the app. The app receives a token that lets it sync, and stores it in its private storage.
- **What is synced:** your games, puzzle progress, puzzle and game ratings with their 30-day history, daily goal and puzzles per day, repeat set, and your buddy, its clothes and the world you chose. The app also sends the device's manufacturer and model name, so you can recognise the device in your account's list of signed-in devices. Device settings such as sound, e-ink mode and language are not synced.
- **Signing out** deletes the token on the device and revokes it on the server. Your games stay on the device.
- **Deleting the account** from the same screen (you confirm with your password, which is sent only to squarechess.org) or at https://squarechess.org/account/delete removes the account and everything synced to it from the server. Games on your device stay.

The squarechess.org privacy notice describes how the account service stores this data. If you turn on leaderboards on squarechess.org (they are off by default), your ratings, puzzles solved and wins against the computer appear there under your username.

## Backups

The same screen can save a backup file of your games, puzzle progress, buddy and settings to a place you choose, using Android's file picker. The app does not upload it.

## Children

Without an account, Square Chess does not collect data from anyone, including children. The account option is in Settings, away from the buddy and sticker screens.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at the same address with a new date.
